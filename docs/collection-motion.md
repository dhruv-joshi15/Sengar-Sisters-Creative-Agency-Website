# Glasshouse collection motion
The seven linked originals are presented as equal-size framed prints, using gentle alternating rotation and a scatter-to-grid entrance. Filtering keeps their layout positions smooth through Motion's `layout`/`AnimatePresence`; the documented transform-based layout and viewport-triggered animation approaches informed the implementation: https://motion.dev/docs/react-layout-animations and https://motion.dev/docs/react-scroll-animations . The work section uses a cached local botanical SVG, a small slowly translating light layer, and bounded glass framing. The primary site palette is unchanged; per-frame mint, peach and violet are highlight accents.

A global fixed SVG with four fine satin-like filaments drifts by transform in both themes and on mobile. Hero perspective, channel leaves, portrait orbit rings, service flower, Results ring, Process timeline, and Contact orbit remain section-specific. All decorative loops stop in reduced-motion mode. The two-ring pointer/keyboard pulse and frame glint are global; existing paper-plane departure remains for contact links. Card links still open the original public post without delaying navigation.
