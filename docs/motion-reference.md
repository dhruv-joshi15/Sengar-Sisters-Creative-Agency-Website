# Folding-screen motion reference
Apple's iPhone Duo product demonstration (https://www.apple.com/iphone-duo/) and Liquid Glass presentation (https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/219/) informed the hinge and blur-to-clear treatment. Implementation is independent SVG/CSS/Framer Motion; no Apple artwork, device assets, or demonstration code is copied.

Two adjacent phone studies open upon native scrolling: the reel phone hinges vertically and the follower phone horizontally. Replay/open-close controls also work with keyboard and touch. Closed screen contents are inert. Reduced motion reveals final counts without the spatial opening or blur. Work cards gently translate and rotate into their grid once visible; hover glass highlights remain bounded. The hero contains two layered adult-proportioned contour sketches with scroll-responsive perspective, not representations of the founders' actual faces.

## Physical hinge correction
Samsung's comparison guide https://www.samsung.com/uk/mobile-phone-buying-guide/samsung-fold-vs-flip/ establishes the Flip's horizontal hinge and book-style Fold's vertical hinge. `HingedPhone.tsx` models fixed and rotating hardware halves, separate inside/outside faces, visible metal hinge, and 180° flap rotation tied continuously to scroll through a damped spring. Flip opens to 220×500; Fold opens to 350×500, from a closed 175×500 footprint. Mobile scales both complete devices together rather than substituting screen effects. The inner screen fades/clears only near full opening; the actual flap remains present throughout. Reduced motion displays open devices.

The hero uses a newly generated anonymous realistic pencil studio illustration. Its anatomy and poses replace the geometric figures; two gently moving masked layers preserve planted feet. It does not depict the founders. The subsequent transparent line-only refinement hit the image-generation usage limit, so the shaded pencil original is rendered with theme-aware blending rather than claiming a pure outline asset.
